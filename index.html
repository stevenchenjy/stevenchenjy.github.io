Research manuscript

Emissions, Affordability, and Evidence under New York City’s Local Law 97

Junyu Chen

Climate rules reach people through buildings, budgets, and institutions. This manuscript examines what New York City’s Local Law 97 records can actually tell us about emissions and affordability, then proposes a careful framework for linking legal design to owner and resident outcomes.

Published conference paper · ASME DMD 2026

Efficiency Over Complexity: Optimal Neural Architectures for Near-Term Forecasting of Parkinsonian Freezing of Gait

Yichen Wu · Junyu Chen · Hao Su

Which model will still work when it meets a new person? This conference paper compares compact models for anticipating Parkinsonian freezing of gait, showing how evaluation on unseen participants changes the apparent performance and the choice of architecture.

Research manuscript

Validation-Selected Hybrid Occupancy Forecasting for Empty-Window Recommendations: An Offline Post-Bin Case Study

Junyu Chen · Chong Li

An accurate forecast is only useful if it supports a sensible decision. This offline building case study blends historical schedules and machine-learning models to identify sustained empty periods, while distinguishing candidate load opportunities from demonstrated energy savings.

Read the paper

Research note

Soft Exosuit with Freezing-of-Gait Detection for Haptic Cueing in Parkinson’s Disease

Junyu Chen · Yichen Wu · Juncheng Zhou · Hao Su

A short research note connects a textile-based pneumatic exosuit with sensor-based detection of freezing of gait. It describes the hardware and compares detection methods, asking how a wearable system could deliver timely physical cues while keeping the wearer’s comfort central.
