Research manuscript

Emissions, Affordability, and Evidence under New York City’s Local Law 97

Junyu Chen

Submitted to International Review of Public Administration · Under review

Submitted to the NYC Mayor’s Office of Climate and Environmental Justice · August 2026

New York City’s Local Law 97 limits emissions from large buildings. This paper examines how its rules, funding, and rent policies connect—and what evidence is needed to judge the effects on building owners and residents.

Published conference paper · Second author

Efficiency Over Complexity: Optimal Neural Architectures for Near-Term Forecasting of Parkinsonian Freezing of Gait

Yichen Wu · Junyu Chen · Hao Su

Published in Proceedings of the 2026 Design of Medical Devices Conference (ASME DMD 2026) · Paper DMD2026-1095

This paper compares compact AI models for predicting freezing of gait: sudden interruptions in walking that affect people with Parkinson’s disease. It tests how well the models work on sensor recordings from participants whose data they have not seen during training.

Author manuscript PDFPublication recordDesign of Medical Devices Conference

Conference paper · Second author

Deep Learning Models for Predicting Parkinson’s Freezing of Gait: Accuracy Comparison of LSTM, CNN, TCN, and Transformer

Yichen Wu · Junyu Chen · Hao Su

Presented at the Duke Medical Robotics Symposium · October 2025

This study compares AI models that predict sudden interruptions in walking for people with Parkinson’s disease, using wearable motion sensors. The comparison weighs prediction accuracy, false alerts, and model size to inform future wearable assistance.

Read the paperDuke symposium

Conference paper · First author

Soft Exosuit with Freezing-of-Gait Detection for Haptic Cueing in Parkinson’s Disease

Junyu Chen · Yichen Wu · Juncheng Zhou · Hao Su

Presented at the Northeast Robotics Colloquium, Cornell University · October 2025

This paper describes a soft, air-powered wearable suit designed to detect sudden interruptions in walking and provide a physical cue at the hip. It combines prototype hardware with a comparison of detection algorithms for Parkinson’s disease.

Read the paperCornell robotics colloquium

Research manuscript

Validation-Selected Hybrid Occupancy Forecasting for Empty-Window Recommendations: An Offline Post-Bin Case Study

Junyu Chen · Chong Li

This offline study uses recorded building data to predict periods likely to remain empty. It combines daily patterns with machine learning to identify times worth investigating for changes to heating, cooling, and lighting; it does not measure actual energy savings.
